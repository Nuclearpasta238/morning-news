## 💻 Tech News

### A run of outages hits Apple, Microsoft, the FAA and others
Apple, Microsoft and the FAA all had major outages within a few days, and some banks and airlines reported disruptions too. On Nov 13 the FAA grounded every flight in the US for about four hours after a failure in a communications network run by L3Harris. Officials say there's no sign of a cyberattack, and DHS Secretary Markwayne Mullin blamed aging systems.
`#Outages` `#Infrastructure` `#FAA` `#Cybersecurity`

### Microsoft outage took down Outlook, Teams and Copilot
On the same day as the FAA ground stop, Microsoft had an outage that it blamed on a network capacity upgrade and a network change that followed it. Outlook, Teams and Copilot were hit, and Microsoft says everything is restored. Both Microsoft and the FAA say the two incidents weren't related.
`#Microsoft` `#Cloud` `#Outages`

### Google says Gmail is fine after outage rumors spread on X
Rumors of a Gmail outage spread on X, so Google posted publicly that Gmail was running normally. TechCrunch called it Google joining "the tech outage trend by announcing it's not having one."
`#Google` `#Gmail` `#SocialMedia`

### US moves to ban imports of new Chinese optical transceivers
Reuters reports that the Trump administration is going ahead with an FCC proposal to ban imports of new models of Chinese-made optical transceivers. These parts are a key component in data center and telecom networks.
`#Policy` `#China` `#Networking` `#TradeRestrictions`

### Apple is closing its Support Community forums
Apple will permanently shut down its user-to-user Support Community forums on November 20. The forums have been a main place for troubleshooting help for many years.

## 🚀 Space Exploration

### Voyager 1 reaches one light-day from Earth today
At about 10:16 UTC today, NASA's Voyager 1 became the first human-made object to reach one light-day from Earth. That's about 16.1 billion miles, or 173 AU. A signal now takes a full day to travel each way, and NASA held a "Shine Bright" celebration last night where people flashed "VGR" in Morse code at the sky.
`#NASA` `#Voyager` `#InterstellarSpace` `#Milestone`

### Starship's upper stage is caught by the tower for the first time
On Flight 15, SpaceX caught Starship's upper stage with the launch tower's arms after a 22-hour orbital mission. The flight also deployed about two dozen Starlink V3 satellites. The Super Heavy booster exploded shortly after liftoff, so the full reusability goal is still unfinished. SpaceX wants reliable reuse in place before Artemis IV in 2028.
`#SpaceX` `#Starship` `#Reusability` `#Artemis`

### ESA's Hera begins close inspection of the asteroid DART hit
Hera has reached the Didymos binary asteroid system ahead of schedule and has started close inspections of Dimorphos, the moonlet NASA's DART spacecraft hit in 2022. Detailed images of Dimorphos are expected around Nov 25, and the Milani and Juventas CubeSats are due to deploy in early 2027.
`#ESA` `#Hera` `#PlanetaryDefense` `#Asteroids`

### Blue Origin delays New Glenn to 2027; NASA is recruiting astronauts
Blue Origin has pushed its next New Glenn launch into 2027. NASA has opened astronaut applications for the first time in two years. Separately, four volunteers came out of NASA's yearlong simulated Mars habitat mission on Nov 10.
`#BlueOrigin` `#NASA` `#Astronauts` `#Mars`

### NASA offers $500K for electric rocket engine ideas
NASA has launched a $500,000 prize competition to speed up work on advanced electric propulsion for satellites and deep-space missions. Separately, the Swift Observatory re-entered Earth's atmosphere on Nov 4 after about two decades studying explosions across the universe.
`#NASA` `#Propulsion` `#Innovation`

## 🎮 Graphics Cards & GPUs

### Nvidia reports a record $115.8B quarter in its first report under the interim CEO
Nvidia's fiscal Q3 2027 revenue was $115.8B, up 103% from a year ago, and data center revenue was $107.6B. Vera Rubin systems brought in close to $20B in their first quarter on sale, which interim CEO Colette Kress called Nvidia's fastest product ramp ever. The company expects Q4 revenue of about $130B, assuming no data center sales to China.
`#Nvidia` `#Earnings` `#VeraRubin` `#DataCenter`

### Nvidia's board rules out bringing back Jensen Huang
Jensen Huang resigned on Nov 12 after disclosing personal conduct the company says broke its code of conduct. Board chair Harvey Jones said on the earnings call that the board "does not intend to reinstate" him, despite a campaign by his allies. Nvidia also set up a new committee to oversee national security and export-control compliance.
`#Nvidia` `#Leadership` `#ExportControls`

### No new GeForce cards in 2026; RTX 50 Super slips to 2027
The Information reports that the RTX 50 Super refresh is design-complete, but Nvidia won't release any new RTX GPUs this year. Memory shortages are the reason, and the refresh is now expected in 2027.
`#Nvidia` `#GeForce` `#RTX50Super` `#Roadmap`

### AMD cuts Radeon supply and raises prices in China
A supply-chain report says AMD sharply cut gaming GPU chip allocations to its partners in China, and the shortage could last into January 2027. AMD has also reportedly raised suggested prices for most Radeon cards in China this quarter, with the biggest increase on the RX 9070. AMD hasn't confirmed either report.
`#AMD` `#Radeon` `#Supply` `#Pricing`

### Code for AMD's next-gen RDNA 5 shows up in Linux patches
GFX13 code, which likely corresponds to RDNA 5, has appeared in new Linux kernel patches. That points to new Radeon GPUs in 2027. AMD also released the Adrenalin 26.11.1 driver to fix two cases where Radeon systems were writing too much data to the system drive.

## 💲 Consumer Tech Pricing

### Memory shortage pushes laptop and phone prices up 15–20%
DRAM prices are still climbing because of AI demand. A 16Gb DDR5 chip cost about $60 on Nov 16, and one list price for a 32GB DDR5 module went from $149 to $239. HP says memory and storage now make up about 35% of what it costs to build a PC, and TrendForce expects PC DRAM contract prices to rise another 13–18% in Q4.
`#RAM` `#MemoryShortage` `#PCs` `#Pricing`

### IDC expects a record $523 average smartphone price
IDC forecasts the average smartphone selling price will hit a record $523 this year, and makers are getting ready for another round of price increases. IDC also expects 2026 Pro flagships to stay at 12GB of RAM instead of moving to 16GB, and says prices are unlikely to drop even if the memory supply stabilizes in 2027.
`#Smartphones` `#IDC` `#Pricing`

### Apple, Amazon, Dell and Samsung have raised prices
Apple has raised Mac and iPad prices by roughly 17–30%, and an iPhone increase is expected. Amazon has raised prices on Echo, Kindle and eero devices by up to 60%. Dell signaled 10–20% increases starting in early November, and Samsung repriced its S and Z series on Nov 1.
`#Apple` `#Amazon` `#Dell` `#Samsung` `#PriceHikes`

### Early Black Friday deals are live, but check the "was" prices
Amazon and Sam's Club already have early sales, and Target, Best Buy and Walmart have started theirs. Consumer Reports warns that some reference prices are inflated. In one case an LG OLED listed against a $3,700 price had mostly been selling for about $2,700.
`#BlackFriday` `#Deals` `#ShoppingTips`

### High-end GPU prices are mixed in Europe
A European price tracker on Nov 14 showed the RTX 5090 dropping €2,840 to €5,609.90. The RTX 5080 and RTX 5060 Ti each rose €100, and the RX 9060 XT rose €75. Worldwide, the RTX 5090 is still selling well above MSRP.
