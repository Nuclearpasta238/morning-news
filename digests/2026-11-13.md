## 🖥️ Tech News

### Jensen Huang resigns as Nvidia CEO; CFO Colette Kress named interim chief
Nvidia's board announced that Jensen Huang has stepped down as CEO, president and board member. CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO while the board looks at permanent candidates, and Harvey C. Jones becomes the company's first board chair. NVDA fell more than 5%, and Kress has called an all-hands meeting for November 18.
**Tags:** `Nvidia` `Leadership` `Semiconductors` `Markets`

### Senate calls Huang and Lutnick to testify as chip export controls tighten
Senate Banking Chair Tim Scott asked Jensen Huang and Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick to testify at separate December hearings about the Nvidia saga, and Lutnick has agreed. The FT reports that Commerce is finalizing rules to stop remote data-center access and chip smuggling into China. Beijing called the "Chinese spy narrative" a pretext, ahead of next week's Trump–Xi meeting.
**Tags:** `Policy` `Export Controls` `China` `Nvidia`

### DOJ moves to seize $1.2B tied to North Korea's $6.3B Binance hack
The Justice Department says a court has authorized it to seize or restrain $1.2B of the $2.7B that Tether and Circle froze after the October 29 hack. That would make it one of the largest crypto recoveries tied to a state-backed attack.
**Tags:** `Crypto` `Cybersecurity` `North Korea` `Law Enforcement`

### Marvell agrees to buy Lumentum in record $115B deal
Marvell agreed to acquire optical-networking maker Lumentum for about $115B, which would be the largest semiconductor deal on record. The move targets the high-speed optical interconnects that AI data centers need.
**Tags:** `M&A` `Semiconductors` `Data Centers` `Optical Networking`

### Agile Robots raises $500M at an $8.5B valuation
Munich-based Agile Robots, which makes humanoids, mobile robots, robotic arms and control software, raised $500M in a SoftBank-led round. Investors keep backing physical-AI and robotics startups.
**Tags:** `Robotics` `Funding` `SoftBank` `Europe`

## 🚀 Space Exploration

### SpaceX Starship Flight 15 launches today with a first-ever double-catch attempt
Starship Flight 15 is scheduled to lift off from Starbase, Texas, today (Nov 13) at about 5:45 p.m. EST / 22:45 UTC. Ship is due to deploy 26 Starlink V3 satellites, and SpaceX will try to catch both the booster and the ship for the first time.
**Tags:** `SpaceX` `Starship` `Starlink` `Launch`

### NASA opens astronaut applications for the first time in two years
NASA is recruiting a new astronaut class, NASASpaceFlight reports (Nov 7). The new astronauts would fly in the Artemis era after Artemis II's crewed lunar flyby in April 2026.
**Tags:** `NASA` `Astronauts` `Artemis` `Careers`

### Four NASA volunteers leave a year-long simulated Mars habitat
After more than a year isolated in a Mars analog habitat, four volunteers have come out. Their data on health, performance and resources will feed into planning for long-duration crewed Mars missions.
**Tags:** `NASA` `Mars` `Human Spaceflight` `Research`

### Swift Observatory reenters after two decades in orbit
NASA's Neil Gehrels Swift Observatory reentered Earth's atmosphere on November 4, ending a roughly 20-year career studying gamma-ray bursts and other high-energy events.
**Tags:** `NASA` `Astronomy` `Spacecraft` `Mission End`

### Blue Origin pushes New Glenn launch into 2027; NASA offers $500K electric-propulsion prize
Blue Origin has moved a planned New Glenn launch into 2027. Separately, NASA opened a $500,000 competition to speed up advanced electric rocket engines for satellites and deep-space missions.
**Tags:** `Blue Origin` `New Glenn` `NASA` `Propulsion`
