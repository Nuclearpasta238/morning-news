## 🚀 Space Exploration

### Starship Flight 15: the ship is caught by the tower after orbit, but the booster is lost
SpaceX launched Starship Flight 15 from Starbase on Nov 13. The upper stage reached orbit, deployed 26 advanced Starlink satellites, and on Saturday became the first Starship to return from orbit and be caught by the tower's "chopsticks." A planned double catch failed when the Super Heavy booster fell into the Gulf of Mexico and exploded.
`#SpaceX` `#Starship` `#Reusability`

Starship is NASA's planned lunar lander. The next Artemis mission will practice docking with Starship and Blue Origin's Blue Moon in Earth orbit, ahead of a lunar landing targeted for as early as 2028. SpaceX expects to start launching Starships from Kennedy Space Center within months.
`#NASA` `#Artemis` `#Moon`

### ESA's Hera arrives at the Didymos asteroid system this month
