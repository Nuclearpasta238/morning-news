## 🚀 Space Exploration

### Starship caught by tower arms after orbital flight
On Nov. 14, SpaceX's Starship upper stage returned from orbit about 22 hours after liftoff and was caught by the launch tower's arms, a first and a big step toward full reusability. The booster exploded shortly after liftoff. NASA's Jared Isaacman called the catch "almost magic."
