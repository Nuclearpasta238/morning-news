**Jensen Huang leaves Nvidia; board reportedly not considering his return**

The stock is down almost 24% this month, and investors are waiting for the first earnings report since he left.

**Uber confirms driver's license data stolen through a breach at its AI vendor**
Hackers took internal credentials from Fireworks AI, a vendor, and used them to reach Uber driver data, including driver's license information. The access started October 7, and Uber sent notices on November 10. State filings so far list several thousand affected drivers, and CNBC put the total at close to 150,000. Uber says it has not seen any active misuse of the data.
